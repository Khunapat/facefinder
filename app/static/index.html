Choose the keepers

Review the suggestions. Originals stay untouched.

How it works

Gemini checked every photo for blur, closed eyes, people cut off by the frame and bad light. Photos it could not check or was unsure about go to Review, never to Rejected. A near-identical copy is rejected only when a clearly better copy is kept. Ticked photos are copied into a new "Keep" folder; the album is not touched.
