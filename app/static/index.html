Who are we looking for?

Add one to three clear photos of each person and give them a name. The name becomes their folder.

Match strictness 0.38 — lower finds more photos but lets in the wrong people

Who gets a folder?

Tick the people you want and type their name. Tap a face to check their photos and pick their best shots. Same person twice? Give both the same name.

Sort by description

Describe the groups in plain words, Thai or English: who they are, what they wear, or what is happening. Gemini puts every photo in the folders it fits. Not sure what to write? Let Gemini suggest groups from the album.

Cull the album

Gemini checked every photo for blur, closed eyes, people cut off by the frame and bad light, and FaceFinder keeps only the best frame of each burst. Nothing is deleted: the keepers are copied into a new folder.
