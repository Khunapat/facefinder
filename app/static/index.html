Choose people

Select people and name their folders.

How it works

Every face in the album is grouped into people. Tap a face to check their photos and pick their best shots. Same person twice? Give both cards the same name and they share a folder. "Looser" makes fewer, bigger groups; "Stricter" makes fewer uncertain matches.

Sort by description

Describe a folder in Thai or English.

How it works

Gemini wrote a short description of every photo during the scan. Sorting matches your folder descriptions against those, so it may miss small details. A photo can go in several folders. Uncertain ones appear in "Check these".

Choose the keepers

Review suggestions. Originals stay untouched.

How it works

Gemini checked every photo for blur, closed eyes, people cut off by the frame and bad light. Photos it could not check or was unsure about go to Review, never to Rejected. A near-identical copy is rejected only when a clearly better copy is kept. Ticked photos are copied into a new "Keep" folder; the album is not touched. "Gentle" only drops clear mistakes; "Strict" keeps only the strongest frames.
