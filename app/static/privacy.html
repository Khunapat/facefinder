Privacy policy

FaceFinder sorts a shared Google Drive photo album into one folder per person. This page says what it reads, what it keeps, where, for how long, and how to delete it.

What FaceFinder reads

When you sign in with Google, FaceFinder asks to read your Google Drive (so it can open the album you paste) and to create files (so it can put copies in new folders in your Drive). It reads only the folders you open in FaceFinder. It never changes, moves or deletes anything in your Drive; an export makes copies. It also reads your email address and name to show who is signed in.

What FaceFinder keeps

  • Your photos are not stored. They are read from Google Drive when needed and then discarded.
  • Face data: for each face, 512 numbers that describe it and where it sits in the photo. This is not a picture. It is used only to match faces in that album, by a face-recognition model that runs inside FaceFinder.
  • Gemini's notes: a short description and quality score for each photo. To make them, a copy of each photo made smaller (768 pixels on the long side) is sent to Gemini through Google Cloud's Vertex AI. Google Cloud's terms say this data is not used to train Google's models.
  • Your names and recent albums: the names you type for people and the albums you opened, kept under a coded form of your email address. Only you see them.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept in a signed cookie in your browser and removed when you sign out.

Where

In FaceFinder's own Google Cloud project (Cloud Run and a Firestore database). Face crops and thumbnails are made when shown and kept only in memory. FaceFinder does not sell or share data, shows no ads and uses no tracking.

How long, and how to delete it

Data is kept until it is deleted. In an open album, use the album menu, then Delete this album's data: this removes its face data, Gemini's notes and your names. People who can open the same Drive folder share its scan, so deleting removes the scan for everyone who uses that folder in FaceFinder; they can scan it again. You can remove FaceFinder's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

FaceFinder's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Drive data is used only to provide FaceFinder's features to you.
